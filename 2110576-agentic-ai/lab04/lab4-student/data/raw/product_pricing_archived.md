## Software subscription

Fleet Manager was licensed per site rather than per robot:

- **Core** — 480,000 THB per site per year.
- **Advanced** — 820,000 THB per site per year.

Per-site licensing was replaced by per-robot licensing in 2025 because it penalised small deployments.

## Support

- **Standard** — included, business hours ICT.
- **Premium** — 10% of hardware value per year, 8-hour response for SEV1.

## Warranty

12 months from commissioning. Extended to 24 months in the current terms.

## Payment terms

Net 30 from invoice for hardware. Late payment carried 1.5% interest per month.
