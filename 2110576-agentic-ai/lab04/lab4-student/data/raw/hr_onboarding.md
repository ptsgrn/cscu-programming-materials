# New Joiner Onboarding

Document HR-001, revision 12. Owner: People Operations, contact people@siamrobotics.example.

## Before day one

The hiring manager files an onboarding ticket in Jira project ONB at least 7 calendar days before the start date. IT provisions the laptop, the Okta account and building access from that ticket. Laptops are shipped to the employee's home address if the start date is remote.

## Day one checklist

1. Collect the access badge from reception, level 3, Rama IV building.
2. Complete Okta enrolment and register a hardware security key. Software TOTP is permitted only for contractors.
3. Sign the confidentiality agreement and the equipment loan form.
4. Attend the 13:00 welcome session in room Chao Phraya.

## First week

New engineers must complete the **Safe Operation of Mobile Robots** course (course code SAF-100) before entering the test floor. The course takes about four hours and is retaken every two years. Access to the test floor is granted by the floor supervisor only after SAF-100 is recorded in the learning system.

Every new joiner is assigned a buddy from a different team. The buddy is not the line manager and has no reporting responsibility; the role exists to answer the questions people are reluctant to ask their manager.

## Probation

Probation lasts 119 days. There is a structured review at day 30, day 60 and day 110. The day 110 review must be submitted in Workday at least 7 days before probation ends; if no review is filed, probation passes by default.

## Equipment

Standard issue is a 14-inch laptop, an external monitor, a keyboard and a mouse. Engineers working on RB-series firmware also receive a USB-CAN adapter and a debug harness. Equipment must be returned within 5 working days of the last day of employment; unreturned equipment is deducted from the final salary payment at book value.
