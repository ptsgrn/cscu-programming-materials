# Leave Policy (ARCHIVED)

**ARCHIVED — superseded by HR-004 revision 7, effective 1 January 2026. Retained for reference only. Do not rely on the figures below.**

Siam Robotics Co., Ltd. — People Operations — document HR-004, revision 3, effective 1 January 2019.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue 12 working days of annual leave per calendar year, accruing at 1.0 days per completed month of service. Employees who have completed seven continuous years of service accrue 18 working days per year instead. Probationary employees may not take annual leave until the probation period of 90 days has ended.

Unused annual leave may be carried into the following year up to a maximum of 3 days. Carried-over days expire on 31 January.

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to 30 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for any absence of two or more consecutive working days.

## Personal and family leave

- Maternity leave: 90 days, of which 45 days are paid at full salary by the company.
- Paternity leave: 5 working days.
- Bereavement leave: 3 working days for an immediate family member.

## Requesting leave

Leave is requested by email to your line manager, copying People Operations. Annual leave of three days or more must be submitted at least 5 working days in advance.

## Public holidays

The company observes 13 public holidays per year.
