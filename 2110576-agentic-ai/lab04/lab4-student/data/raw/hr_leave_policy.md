# Leave Policy

Siam Robotics Co., Ltd. — People Operations — document HR-004, revision 7, effective 1 January 2026.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees accrue 15 working days of annual leave per calendar year, accruing at 1.25 days per completed month of service. Employees who have completed five continuous years of service accrue 20 working days per year instead. Probationary employees accrue leave from their start date but may not take annual leave until the probation period of 119 days has ended.

Unused annual leave may be carried into the following year up to a maximum of 5 days. Carried-over days expire on 31 March. The company does not pay out unused annual leave except on termination of employment.

## Sick leave

Employees may take up to 30 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. A medical certificate is required for any absence of three or more consecutive working days. Sick leave does not accrue and does not carry over.

## Personal and family leave

- Maternity leave: 98 days, of which 45 days are paid at full salary by the company.
- Paternity leave: 15 working days, to be taken within 90 days of the birth.
- Bereavement leave: 5 working days for an immediate family member, 2 days otherwise.
- Ordination leave: 15 days, once per period of employment.

## Requesting leave

All leave is requested in Workday under **Time Off → Request**. Annual leave of three days or more must be submitted at least 10 working days in advance. Requests are approved by the direct line manager; if the manager does not respond within 3 working days, the request escalates automatically to the department head.

Leave taken without an approved request is recorded as unpaid absence and, after three occurrences in a rolling 12-month window, triggers a formal review under the disciplinary procedure described in HR-011.

## Public holidays

The company observes 16 public holidays per year, published each December by People Operations. Employees required to work on a public holiday receive either a substitute day off or 2x pay, chosen by the employee.
