# Approved Suppliers

Document OPS-017, revision 22. Owner: Supply Chain. Reviewed quarterly.

| Component | Supplier | Part number | Lead time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Drive motor | Nidec Precision (JP) | NP-4408-48 | 12 weeks |
| Safety lidar | Hokuyo (JP) | UST-20LX-SR | 16 weeks |
| Battery cell, LFP | EVE Energy (CN) | LF105 | 10 weeks |
| Battery cell, LTO | Toshiba (JP) | SCiB-23Ah | 20 weeks |
| Main compute module | Advantech (TW) | MIO-5375 | 8 weeks |
| Castor assembly | Thai Precision Works (TH) | TPW-C120 | 4 weeks |

The safety lidar is single sourced. This is a known and accepted risk: it is the only SIL 2 rated unit that fits the RB-7 deck height, and qualifying a second source is an 18-month programme. Safety stock is held at 40 units, which is roughly one quarter of production.

## Qualification

A new supplier of a safety-critical part requires: an on-site audit, 30 sample parts through incoming inspection, a 500-hour life test, and sign-off from both Quality and the responsible design engineer. Nothing about this process can be compressed for schedule reasons.

## Ordering

Purchase orders above 500,000 THB require two approvals. Above 5,000,000 THB the CFO approves. Orders are placed through Coupa; email purchase orders are not valid and will not be paid.

## Escalation

Late deliveries on critical components are escalated to the Supply Chain lead on day one of the delay, not at the promised date. The 20-week LTO cell lead time means an RB-9 build slot missed in Q1 cannot be recovered inside the same year.
