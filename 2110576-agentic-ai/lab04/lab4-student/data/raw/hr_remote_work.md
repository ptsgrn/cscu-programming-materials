# Remote and Hybrid Work

Document HR-019, revision 3, effective 1 April 2026. Supersedes the temporary remote work guidance issued in 2024.

## Default arrangement

Office-based roles work from the Bangkok office a minimum of two days per week. Teams choose which days, but the whole team must anchor on at least one shared day so that planning and design discussions happen in person. Team anchor days are published in the team charter.

Roles that require physical access to hardware — test engineering, manufacturing, field service — are on site four or five days per week. This is a safety and equipment-access constraint, not a performance one.

## Fully remote roles

Fully remote employment is approved case by case by the department head and People Operations together. Approval requires that the role has no recurring hardware dependency and that the employee has completed probation. Fully remote employees must be in a time zone within three hours of Indochina Time (ICT, UTC+7).

## Working from abroad

Employees may work from outside Thailand for up to 20 working days per calendar year with prior approval. Approval is required because of tax residency and export control rules, not convenience. Requests go to People Operations at least 15 working days in advance and must state the country and the exact dates. Work from a country on the restricted list maintained in SEC-002 is not permitted under any circumstances.

## Equipment and expenses

The company provides a one-time home office allowance of 15,000 THB, claimable after probation. Internet costs are not reimbursed. Ergonomic assessments are available on request through People Operations.

## Meetings

Any meeting with at least one remote participant is treated as a remote meeting: everyone joins from their own device with their own camera. Half-in-room, half-on-screen meetings systematically disadvantage remote participants and are explicitly discouraged.
