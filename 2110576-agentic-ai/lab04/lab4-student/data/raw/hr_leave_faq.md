# Leave — Frequently Asked Questions

Maintained by the People Operations helpdesk. This page answers process questions. For entitlements, allowances, accrual rates and carry-over limits, always read the current leave policy (HR-004) rather than this page — the numbers change and this page does not.

**My manager has not approved my leave request. What do I do?**
Wait three working days. If there is still no response the request escalates automatically. If it has not escalated, raise a helpdesk ticket.

**Can I cancel approved leave?**
Yes, in Workday, up to the day before the leave starts. Cancelling on the day requires a manager override.

**Does leave show in my payslip?**
Paid leave does not appear as a separate line. Unpaid absence appears as a deduction in the following month.

**I am on a fixed-term contract. Do the same rules apply?**
Fixed-term employees accrue leave on the same basis as permanent employees, pro-rated to the contract length.

**Do I lose leave if I change teams?**
No. Accrued leave follows you within the company.

**What about leave during notice period?**
Annual leave during a notice period requires department head approval, because it interacts with handover obligations.

**Who do I ask about the number of days I am entitled to?**
Check HR-004, or your Workday balance, which is authoritative. The helpdesk does not quote entitlements from memory.

**Is there a company-wide shutdown?**
No. The company does not close between the public holidays listed each December.
