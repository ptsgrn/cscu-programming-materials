# Commercial Terms and Pricing

Document PRD-200, revision 9. Owner: Commercial team. All prices exclude VAT and are quoted in Thai Baht.

## Hardware

| Item | List price (THB) |
|---|---|
| RB-7 robot | 1,450,000 |
| RB-9 robot | 1,890,000 |
| DK-2 dock | 210,000 |
| DK-3 dock (cold chain) | 340,000 |
| Spare battery pack, RB-7 | 145,000 |

Volume discount is 8% from 10 units, 14% from 25 units and negotiated above 50 units. Discounts apply to robots only, never to docks or spares.

## Software subscription

Fleet Manager is licensed per robot per year:

- **Core** — 96,000 THB. Task assignment, dashboards, 13-month telemetry retention.
- **Advanced** — 156,000 THB. Adds multi-site routing, the simulation sandbox and the partner API tier.

Subscription is mandatory for the first three years. Robots without an active subscription still operate but do not receive firmware updates, including safety updates.

## Support

- **Standard** — included. Business hours ICT, next business day response.
- **Premium** — 12% of hardware value per year. 24/7, 4-hour response for SEV1, on-site within 24 hours in Bangkok, Chonburi and Rayong.

## Warranty

24 months from commissioning, covering parts and labour. Battery packs are warranted to retain 80% capacity for 24 months or 1,500 cycles, whichever comes first. Warranty is void if the robot is operated outside its rated environment — running an RB-7 in cold storage is the most common void reason.

## Payment terms

Net 45 from invoice for hardware, annual in advance for subscription. Late payment carries 1.25% interest per month. Pilot deployments of three robots or fewer may be billed monthly for up to six months.
