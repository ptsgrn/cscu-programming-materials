# Glossary

Short definitions of terms used across Siam Robotics documentation. For anything operational, follow the document referenced in the entry.

**AMR** — Autonomous Mobile Robot. Our RB-series products. Distinct from an AGV, which follows a fixed physical guide.

**Anchor day** — the weekday a team agrees to be in the office together.

**Candidate set** — the robots eligible for a task before the coordinator scores them.

**Commissioning** — the point at which a robot is accepted at a customer site. Warranty starts here, not at shipment.

**Dock** — the charging station. DK-1 for RB-5, DK-2 for RB-7, DK-3 for RB-9.

**Duty cycle** — the mix of driving, idling and lifting used to quote runtime. Runtime figures are meaningless without one.

**Fleet coordinator** — the service that assigns tasks to robots and maintains the fleet's shared state.

**IC** — Incident Commander. Owns decisions during an incident. See ENG-007.

**ICT** — Indochina Time, UTC+7. Every timestamp in our documentation is ICT unless stated otherwise.

**Opportunity charging** — sending an idle robot to a dock rather than waiting for a low battery.

**PDPA** — Thailand's Personal Data Protection Act. Drives the retention and deletion rules in ENG-052.

**Quorum** — the majority of coordinator nodes required to agree before task assignments are valid.

**SEV1 / SEV2 / SEV3** — incident severity levels. See ENG-007.

**SIL 2** — a functional safety integrity level. Our safety lidar is rated to it; the depth cameras are not, which is why the lidar alone defines the protective stop.

**Soak** — the period a release runs under synthetic traffic in staging before promotion.

**State of charge** — remaining battery, as a percentage.

**Transition zone** — the buffered area between cold storage and ambient, used to manage condensation on RB-9 units.
