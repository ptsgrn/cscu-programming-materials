Codes in the 5000 range are coordination faults, codes in the 8000 range are firmware faults, and codes in the 9000 range originate outside the robot.

If a code does not appear here, it was raised by a third-party component and should be searched in the vendor documentation rather than internally.

This index is regenerated weekly from the source tree. Do not edit it by hand; edits are overwritten.
