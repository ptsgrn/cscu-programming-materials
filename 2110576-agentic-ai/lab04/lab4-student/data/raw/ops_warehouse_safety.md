# Warehouse Safety Rules

Document OPS-003, revision 8. Owner: EHS. Applies to every site where Siam Robotics equipment operates, including customer sites.

## Before entering a robot zone

Everyone entering an active robot zone must have completed SAF-100 within the last two years and must wear a high-visibility vest. Visitors are escorted at all times by a badged employee; there are no exceptions for executives or customers.

## Right of way

People always have right of way. Robots yield. Staff must not, however, rely on that: never step into an aisle from behind a rack without looking, because the safety lidar has a 270 degree field of view and a robot approaching from the blind sector needs 350 mm to stop.

## Emergency stop

Every robot has two red emergency stop buttons, one on each side of the deck. Pressing either cuts drive power immediately and applies the brakes. A robot in emergency stop must be reset physically at the robot by a trained operator; it cannot be cleared from the fleet console.

Zone-wide emergency stop is triggered by the mushroom buttons at each aisle entrance and by the pull cords along the pick faces. Zone stop halts every robot in the zone within 500 ms.

## Manual movement

A robot may be pushed manually only after emergency stop is engaged and the drive release lever is set to neutral. Pushing a robot without releasing the drive damages the gearbox and is the second most common warranty rejection.

## Incidents

Any contact between a robot and a person is reported within one hour, regardless of injury, using the EHS incident form. Contact incidents are automatically SEV1 under ENG-007 and the zone is stopped until an EHS officer clears it.

Near misses are reported too. A site that reports zero near misses is not a safe site, it is a site that is not reporting.

## Housekeeping

Aisles must be kept clear to the full 900 mm minimum width. Pallets, wrap and cages left in an aisle force robots into recovery behaviour, which is slow and, repeated often enough, is what makes an operations team decide the robots do not work.
